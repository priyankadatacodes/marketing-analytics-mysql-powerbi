## Interpretation notes
- **ROI and every other ratio is a ratio of sums.** Portfolio ROI = (Σ revenue − Σ spend) ÷ Σ spend = −0.6126. Adding the 220 campaign-level ROIs gives −121.86, which is meaningless; this is what the page-3 Total row showed. Per-campaign ROI is valid only on one row per `campaign_id` (`vw_campaign_performance`), where ROI = ROAS − 1 holds exactly.
- **ROI is revenue-based.** There is no margin or cost-of-goods data, so ROI measures revenue return on spend, not profit return; a margin-adjusted ROI cannot be calculated from this data.
- **ROAS and ROI use revenue, not profit.** Without margin data, ROAS < 1 means recorded sales are below spend, not necessarily that the business loses money.
- **CAC and LTV use different populations in two places.** The headline "Average LTV" averages all 9,985 customers while CAC divides by the 3,075 acquired customers; the channel LTV:CAC ratios use acquired customers for both.
- **The retention flag is not time-windowed** (any return after day 30, at any later date) and counts customers with no signup date as not retained; the cohort matrix is the more conservative view.
- **Repeat-customer % is nearly uniform** (97–99%), so it does not discriminate between channels.
- **`revenue_retention_pct` (`11`)** is revenue after day 90 divided by revenue in the first 90 days; values of 580–715% reflect the ratio definition, not a retention rate in the usual sense.
